## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an ultra-premium, on-demand home services marketplace tailored for the contemporary MENA region with deep Egyptian contextual resonance. Moving far beyond utilitarian service directories, the brand personality projects architectural precision, uncompromising trust, bespoke craftsmanship, and effortless reliability. 

The aesthetic is Modern Tactile Minimalism: deep obsidian structural surfaces, radiant solar-gold callouts reminiscent of polished brass hardware and Egyptian sunlight, paired with soft alabaster canvas foundations. Micro-borders, crisp hairline geometry, layered pill badges, and refined ambient shadows establish a tactile luxury layer that reassures high-net-worth homeowners while celebrating verified trade masters (plumbers, master electricians, HVAC technicians, bespoke carpenters). The UI is strictly bilingual-ready (LTR/RTL balanced), maintaining structural harmony and optical weight whether experienced in English or Arabic script.

## Colors

The palette leverages high-contrast architectural values to build instant trust and focus:

- **Primary Canvas & Accents (`#0B132B`, `#1C2541`):** Deep Obsidian and Slate Navy. Used for authoritative headline elements, premium active containers, primary CTAs, high-value status bars, and luxury service tier cards.
- **Secondary Energy Accent (`#F59E0B`, `#D97706`):** Solar Amber / Burnished Gold. Highlights urgent dispatch triggers, active booking slots, price highlights, and tactile primary interactions without cheapening into utility yellow.
- **Tertiary Assurance (`#10B981`, `#059669`):** Deep Oasis Emerald. Dedicated exclusively to background check verifications, warranty stamps, live master craftsman availability indicators, and booking confirmations.
- **Surface Foundations (`#F8FAFC`, `#F1F5F9`, `#FFFFFF`):** Alabaster and Soft Cream-Gray base canvas. Provides a calm, pristine stage for high-density service menus and craftsman profiles.
- **Borders & Micro-Dividers (`#E2E8F0`, `#CBD5E1`):** Tactile hairline separators that define cards and modules with physical, blueprint-like precision.

## Typography

The type scale combines the technical, geometric exactness of **Space Grotesk** for display headlines with the rhythmic, highly legible humanist clarity of **Manrope** for service metadata, pricing, reviews, and body narratives.

- **Headlines:** Use Space Grotesk to evoke structural architecture, technical precision, and modern craftsmanship. Letter-spacing is set tight (`-0.02em`) on sizes above 24px.
- **Body & Labels:** Manrope carries all transactional and booking flows. It maintains exceptional legibility across dense mobile screens, Arabic numeral sets, and bidirectional Arabic/English parity.
- **Currency & Numerics:** Tabular figures are enforced on pricing, service ratings, and arrival ETAs to maintain alignment during real-time updates.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is anchored in an 8pt base grid optimized for one-thumb mobile interaction, scaling seamlessly into tablet booking dashboards:

- **Mobile Viewports (<640px):** Single-column layout with strict 16px (`1rem`) outer margins. Touch targets maintain a minimum vertical height of 48px with 8px internal gaps.
- **Tablet & Split-Panels (640px–1024px):** 6-column layout with 24px gutters. Master list stays pinned while craftsman portfolio and dynamic booking estimates occupy the responsive secondary pane.
- **Desktop (1024px+):** Max layout container capped at 1280px with a 12-column structure for dispatcher and corporate property accounts.
- **Bilingual Rhythms:** Symmetrical lateral margins and gutters guarantee identical physical spacing when toggled between LTR (English) and RTL (Arabic), preventing layout shifting across bidirectional states.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth is tactile and subtle, rejecting cartoonish blur in favor of physical, architectural stacking:

- **Base Layer (0dp):** Canvas foundation `#F8FAFC` layered beneath `#FFFFFF` primary modules.
- **Micro-Border Framework:** Every surface card combines a hairline border (`1px solid #E2E8F0`) with a faint ambient shadow (`0 2px 4px -1px rgba(11, 19, 43, 0.04), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(11, 19, 43, 0.03)`).
- **Interactive Elevated Cards:** Hover/press states translate up by -2px with expanded obsidian-tinted diffusion: `0 12px 24px -4px rgba(11, 19, 43, 0.08), 0 4px 8px -2px rgba(11, 19, 43, 0.04)`.
- **Floating Modals & Bottom Sheets:** Frosted backdrops (`backdrop-filter: blur(16px); background: rgba(11, 19, 43, 0.4)`) anchoring solid white panels with deep containment shadows (`0 -8px 32px rgba(11, 19, 43, 0.12)`).

## Shapes

The shape hierarchy balances modern architectural solidity with comfortable handheld ergonomics:

- **Standard Containers (`rounded-lg`, 16px):** Primary service cards, booking review cards, technician detail modules.
- **Interactive Controls (`rounded`, 8px to 12px):** Inputs, dropdown toggles, service selector tabs.
- **Status Pills & Micro Indicators (`rounded-full`, 9999px):** Verification badges, category chips, rating bubbles, and live countdown tags use perfect capsule/pill geometry to establish clear visual differentiation against structural square cards.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Book Now / Confirm Service):** Solid `#0B132B` background with `#FFFFFF` text. Micro-border of `1px solid #1C2541`. Height: 52px on mobile. Active states apply a subtle scale (0.98) and amber glow highlight (`0 0 0 2px #F59E0B`).
- **Urgent / Express Dispatch:** Vibrant `#F59E0B` background with `#0B132B` bold typography.
- **Secondary / Ghost:** Transparent background, `1.5px solid #CBD5E1` border, `#0B132B` text, shifting to soft `#F1F5F9` on touch.

### Chips & Verification Badges
- **Craftsman Verified Pill:** Deep `#10B981` (10% tint background: `#ECFDF5`), `#065F46` label, paired with an emerald checkmark glyph and subtle `1px solid rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.2)` edge.
- **Filter & Category Chips:** Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), padded with 12px horizontal and 8px vertical. Default state in `#FFFFFF` with `#E2E8F0` border. Active state shifts to `#0B132B` with white text.

### Cards (Service & Craftsman Profile)
- Crisp white background (`#FFFFFF`) sitting on `#F8FAFC`.
- Outlined with `1px solid #E2E8F0`.
- Top-right (or top-left in RTL) houses an absolute-positioned rating pill (`#FEF3C7` background, `#D97706` star and typography).
- Includes craftsman thumbnail with an emerald online status dot, trade specialization subtitle, and dynamic price estimate in bold Space Grotesk.

### Input Fields & Selectors
- Background `#FFFFFF`, border `1.5px solid #E2E8F0`, rounded 12px, height 48px.
- Focus state transforms the border to `#0B132B` with an ambient glow (`box-shadow: 0 0 0 3px rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.2)`).
- RTL-native layout: placeholder and input adornment icons dynamically flip alignment according to the active locale without layout breakages.

### State & Tracking Timeline
- Micro-stepping component displaying request progression (*Booked*, *Assigned*, *En Route*, *Working*, *Completed*).
- Completed segments feature solid obsidian stems; live in-progress step pulses with `#F59E0B` solar amber ring indicators.
